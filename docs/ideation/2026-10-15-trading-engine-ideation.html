1. Canonical Dual-Asset Quote Bridge & Ingestion Normalizer

Axis: Execution & Pricing Integrity
Conf: 98% Complexity: Medium

Establishes a unified translation layer at the ingestion boundary that simultaneously ingests live quotes, spreads, tick increments, and FX exchange rates for both the underlying index/stock and its paired European 3x ETP. Underlying candles are utilized strictly for directional indicator math, while position sizing, ATR-based risk distances, and order execution are computed natively in ETP price coordinates and currency.

Articulated Basis

direct: trading_system/agents/risk_manager.py:166-173 computes entry_price = metrics.get('close'), stop_loss, and position_size_shares on underlying prices ($500 QQQ), which trading_system/execution/position_monitor.py:135-154 checks against ETP market prices (£15 QQQ3), triggering immediate false liquidations.

Strategic Rationale

Resolves the single most critical structural bug in the repository. Without this bridge, every live order buys <£10 of an asset or instantly stops out on the first price tick. Normalizing at the ingestion boundary makes all downstream agents, risk rules, and monitors permanently mathematically coherent.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Increases market ingestion requests from 1 to 2 API calls per asset pair, requiring lightweight caching to stay well inside broker rate limits.

2. Avionic Flight Envelope: Realized Path-Variance Budgeting for 3x Volatility Drag

Axis: Mathematical Risk Engineering
Conf: 95% Complexity: Medium

Replaces static linear stop-losses with an Avionic Flight Envelope that models quadratic variance drag (\(\int \sigma^2 dt\)) in 3x daily rebalanced ETPs. The engine tracks an accumulating "Variance Budget": if an asset enters prolonged sideways chop where mathematical drag exceeds expected delta, the flight envelope triggers an autonomous Alpha-Floor exit, overriding bullish LLM conviction and liquidating the position before compounding decay destroys capital.

Articulated Basis

external: Avellaneda & Zhang (2010), Path-Dependence of Leveraged ETF Returns, proving quadratic drag tracks \(-3.0 \cdot V_t\) for \(eta=3\); direct: trading_system/agents/risk_manager.py:78 relies on 1x unscaled ATR stops oblivious to path variance.

Strategic Rationale

3x leveraged products are non-Markovian: price level alone does not reflect position health. A trade that oscillates near breakeven for 4 days loses 4–8% in ETP value purely to mathematical drag. Budgeting variance terminates dead-money positions dynamically.

Tradeoffs & Costs

May occasionally exit right before a delayed breakout if choppy price action prematurely consumes the path-variance budget.

3. Pre-Mortem Invalidation Sentinel: Fast Adversarial Risk Gating

Axis: Multi-Agent Deliberation Architecture
Conf: 92% Complexity: Low

Re-architects System 2 multi-agent deliberation by stripping LLMs of slow, hallucinatory directional price forecasting and re-anchoring them strictly as an adversarial "Pre-Mortem Invalidation Sentinel". High-speed statistical models (Laya NLI, Connors 3-RSI, ADX) determine entry alpha; the candidate then passes to a lightweight sub-2-second adversarial LLM auditor whose sole job is to prove an immediate disqualifier (e.g. corporate earnings within 48h, antitrust/macro releases, abnormal bid-ask spread widening). If no fatal disqualifier is proven, the order clears immediately.

Articulated Basis

direct: trading_system/agents/debate_engine.py:24-33 blocks execution for 15–45s while generating speculative narrative arguments; external: FinRobot / TradingAgents principle of "Computation vs. Narration" (deterministic code drives quantitative triggers; LLMs provide qualitative boundary checks).

Strategic Rationale

LLMs possess near-zero directional edge on microsecond technical price bounces, but excel at unstructured qualitative risk identification. Re-scoping the LLM to an adversarial veto slashes execution latency from 30s to <2s while filtering out catastrophic exogenous landmines.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Relies on accurate qualitative inputs (news/catalyst dates) fed into the prompt; overly pessimistic prompts could veto valid high-volatility breakouts.

4. Asymmetric Session Protocol: Intraday Inverse Hedging & 16:20 Gap Evacuation

Axis: Market Microstructure & Session Timing
Conf: 94% Complexity: Low

Enforces an asymmetric holding rule across directional instruments: while 3x Long positions (QQQ3) can be held as multi-day tactical swings (capped at 4 days), 3x Inverse/Short positions (QQQS) are restricted strictly to intraday tactical overlays with mandatory 16:20 UTC liquidation. Simultaneously, any open long position that has not secured breakeven stop protection (+1R) prior to the 16:30 UTC London close is automatically flattened, eliminating unhedged overnight gap risk while London is shut and US markets trade post-market earnings.

Articulated Basis

direct: trading_system/execution/position_monitor.py:26-54 models longs and shorts with identical multi-day holding states; external: Avellaneda & Zhang (2010), demonstrating that inverse -3x ETPs suffer double the volatility drag penalty combined with equity risk premia, making overnight holding statistically toxic.

Strategic Rationale

European ETPs are frozen for 4.5 hours while US cash equities continue trading into after-hours earnings. If a tech company reports disastrous earnings at 21:05 UTC, an LSE-listed ETP will gap down 15–25% at 08:00 UTC London open, blowing right past any software stop-loss. Intraday flattening completely removes this gap risk.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Closes positions that might have gapped favorably overnight; limits short-side profits strictly to same-day session moves.

5. Zero-Drag Currency-Native Routing & Fractional Position Streaming

Axis: Broker Execution & Cost Optimization
Conf: 96% Complexity: Low

Upgrades the instrument selector and risk manager to dynamically route trades to the specific ETP share class matching the broker account's base currency (e.g. selecting GBP-denominated 3USL/QQ3 on LSE instead of USD QQQ3), and refactors position sizing from rounded integer shares to continuous fractional monetary notional. This completely eliminates Trading 212's 0.15% spot FX conversion fee (saving 0.30% round-trip) and eliminates the max(1.0, round(shares)) bug that forces massive over-allocation on smaller balances.

Articulated Basis

direct: trading_system/agents/risk_manager.py:148-151 forces integer rounding (shares = max(1.0, round(raw_shares, 2))), breaking sizing on accounts under £5,000; external: Trading 212 Fee Schedule (0.15% currency conversion fee on cross-currency trades).

Strategic Rationale

On Connors 3-RSI tactical mean-reversion trades with an expected edge of 1.0–1.5%, paying 0.30% in round-trip FX fees consumes 20–30% of total strategy alpha. Eliminating this friction directly injects hundreds of basis points back into net portfolio returns.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Requires cataloging all multi-currency share classes (USD, GBP, EUR) for each leveraged ETP in LeveragedUniverse.

6. End-of-Day Rebalance Liquidity Harvester (The 16:15 UTC MOC Flow Engine)

Axis: Alpha Strategy & Order Flow
Conf: 90% Complexity: Medium

Transforms daily leveraged ETP rebalancing from a spread hazard into an active execution and momentum engine. 3x ETP issuers are legally mandated to rebalance their swap/futures books at the market close to maintain constant 3x leverage, creating predictable, non-discretionary order flow into the 16:30 UTC London close. The engine models this expected institutional volume (\(Q_{ ext{rebal}} = (eta - 1) rac{\Delta S}{S} ext{AUM}\)) to enter momentum continuation trades at 16:15 UTC on strong trend days, crossing into maximum daily liquidity and exiting at the market-on-close auction without overnight risk.

Articulated Basis

external: Cheng & Madhavan (2009), The Dynamics of Leveraged and Inverse Exchange-Traded Funds, documenting predictable market-on-close liquidity surges and momentum continuation caused by mechanical index rebalancing; direct: trading_system/orchestrator.py:5-7 currently treats rebalancing purely as a decay hazard to avoid.

Strategic Rationale

Trading alongside mandatory, non-discretionary institutional order flow provides the cleanest statistical tailwind in financial markets, while allowing the engine to capture alpha with zero overnight holding risk and near-zero volatility drag.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Trade window is narrow (16:15–16:30 UTC); requires reliable intraday volume and return telemetry.

7. Fail-Fast Startup Attestation & Reactive Pub/Sub Event Telemetry

Axis: Infrastructure Resilience & Observability
Conf: 94% Complexity: Medium

Replaces fragile file-based state synchronization (active_trades.json, scratch/rsi3_scan.json, notifications.jsonl) with a thread-safe in-memory Pub/Sub event bus that streams live execution events to signals_dashboard.html via Server-Sent Events (SSE). Concurrently establishes a strict ProductionEnvironmentValidator that halts engine execution loudly if live broker credentials, L1 market quotes, or authenticated model endpoints fail, permanently eliminating silent degradation into synthetic candles and mock debates.

Articulated Basis

direct: trading_system/orchestrator.py:313-316 silently falls back to dry-run simulation when broker checks fail; trading_system/execution/position_monitor.py:83, 107 performs synchronous file I/O locks on local JSON files during active trade ticks.

Strategic Rationale

Silent mock fallbacks are the ultimate production trap—they create the illusion that the engine is trading live when it is actually evaluating toy data. Pairing a fail-fast barrier with an event bus provides zero-latency observability and unshakeable operational reliability.

Tradeoffs & Costs

Halts engine execution on missing dependencies or broken API keys rather than attempting best-effort simulation.
